Physics, drawn from your data.

Folders form districts. File size sets each object’s mass. Recently modified files create stronger currents, and observed edits send out ripples. Gravity and drift are a playful simulation of that metadata.

The map uses file names, sizes and timestamps. Press E near a file, or aim at a crystal up to 90 units away and fire to open it; contents are read on demand and stay on this computer. Hidden files, dependencies and generated folders are excluded. Connected folders refresh every five seconds. With the snapshot picker, reselect your folder to include changes. Nothing is uploaded.

Physics, drawn from your data.

Move the reticle independently of the ship, then click or press Q to send a probe to a locked file within 160 units. Press C to center it on your forward vector. Press E near a file to open it; contents are read on demand and stay on this computer. Hidden files, dependencies and generated folders are excluded. The map refreshes every five seconds.
